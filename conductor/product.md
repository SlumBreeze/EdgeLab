# Initial Concept
EdgeLab v3: A professional-grade sports betting intelligence platform designed to eliminate emotional bias using AI-driven research and real-time market data to identify +EV plays.

# Product Definition

## Target Audience
- **Professional Handicappers:** Individuals requiring deep data analysis, rigorous discipline enforcement, and automated research workflows to maintain a professional edge.
- **Data Scientists:** Advanced users building or refining betting models who value granular data, custom logic parameters, and transparency in AI reasoning.

## Core Value Proposition
EdgeLab acts as a specialized "operating system" for handicappers. It replaces emotional decision-making with a mathematical and AI-driven "Veto System" that audits every potential play against market efficiency and qualitative data.

## Key Features & Functional Requirements
- **Rigorous Veto System:**
    - **Price Veto:** Automated rejection of lines with excessive juice (e.g., > -160) to protect long-term ROI.
    - **Narrative Audits:** AI-driven cross-referencing of public betting "narratives" against verified injury reports and lineup data.
    - **Sharp/Soft Delta Analysis:** Mandatory price comparison between sharp market-makers (Pinnacle) and retail books to identify mathematical inefficiencies.
- **Custom AI Personas:** An interface allowing advanced users to configure the "Stoic Handicapper" agent's parameters, including risk tolerance, edge requirements, and sport-specific focus.
- **Bankroll Management:** Automated unit sizing via Kelly Criterion, integrated with multi-book balance tracking.

## Success Metrics
- **ROI (Return on Investment):** The primary measure of the platform's ability to identify profitable opportunities.
- **Veto Accuracy:** Monitoring the effectiveness of the AI in correctly flagging losing scenarios ("Pass" recommendations) and identifying "traps."

## Visual Identity
- **Bloomberg/Terminal Inspired:** A minimalist, data-dense, monochromatic layout designed for maximum informational throughput and professional efficiency, avoiding the "high-energy" clutter of consumer betting apps.
